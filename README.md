## Customer Sales & Behavior Insights

An end-to-end data analytics project using Python, MySQL, and Power BI to explore customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, revenue trends, and subscription patterns.

## Project Overview

Businesses collect customer data every day, but transforming that data into meaningful insights is essential for understanding purchasing patterns and supporting informed decisions.

This project analyzes 3,900 customer records across 18 attributes to explore customer spending, product performance, category-wise revenue, customer demographics, and subscription behavior.

The project covers the analytics workflow, from data cleaning and feature engineering to SQL-based analysis and interactive Power BI visualization.

## Objectives

- Analyze revenue and sales across product categories.
- Understand purchasing patterns across different age groups.
- Examine customer subscription behavior.
- Compare average purchase amounts and customer review ratings.
- Explore discount usage and purchasing frequency.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business-oriented analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:-** Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **Pandas:-** Data manipulation and feature engineering
- **MySQL:-** Data querying and business analysis
- **SQL:-** Joins, subqueries, aggregations, CTEs, CASE statements, and window functions
- **Power BI:-** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook:-** Data analysis workflow

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:-** Loaded the customer dataset and examined its structure.
2. **Data Cleaning:-** Handled missing review ratings using category-wise median values and standardized column names.
3. **Feature Engineering:-** Created age groups and prepared additional customer-related features.
4. **SQL Analysis:-** Analyzed revenue, product ratings, discount usage, subscription status, and customer segments.
5. **Dashboard Development:-** Created interactive visualizations and filters in Power BI.
6. **Insight Exploration:-** Used the dashboard to compare customer and sales metrics across different categories.

## Key Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:-** Average purchase amount, average review rating, and customer count.
- **Revenue by Category:-** Compares revenue across clothing, accessories, footwear, and outerwear.
- **Sales by Category:-** Shows category-level sales distribution.
- **Subscription Analysis:-** Displays the proportion of subscribers and non-subscribers.
- **Age Group Analysis:-** Compares revenue and sales across customer age groups.
- **Interactive Filters:-** Explore results by subscription status, gender, product category, and shipping type.

## Business Questions Explored

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How does customer spending vary across age groups?
- What is the distribution of subscribers and non-subscribers?
- Which products receive higher average review ratings?
- How frequently are discounts applied to different products?
- How do purchasing patterns differ between customer segments?
